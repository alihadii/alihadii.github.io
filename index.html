02 / aims

Aims & goals

My work sits where data-driven models meet physics-based building simulation — aiming for methods that are accurate, interpretable, and robust enough to trust once they leave the lab.

  • G1
    Reliable fault diagnosisDetect and disambiguate HVAC faults early enough to act on, with models that generalise beyond their training data.
  • G2
    Climate-resilient, efficient buildings & data centersQuantify how building, HVAC, and cooling choices perform under future-climate uncertainty — including the growing energy load of data centers.
  • G3
    Trustworthy forecastingDeliver probabilistic, leakage-safe forecasts for solar and demand signals that expose their own uncertainty.
  • G4
    Deployment-ready AIPush explainability and agentic methods toward tools that survive contact with real buildings and edge hardware.
